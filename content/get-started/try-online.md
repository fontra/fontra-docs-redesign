<h2 id="open">Open it</h2>

Go to <a href="https://try.fontrahive.com">try.fontrahive.com</a>. The first time, Fontra opens on MutatorSans, a small variable font to play with; a short tour of the editor is offered. The next times, it opens the font you last worked on.

Everything you see is the same Fontra as <a href="/how-tos/installation/installing-fontra-pak/">Fontra Pak</a> and a Fontra server: the editor, the <a href="/reference/">Font overview</a>, Font Info, the settings. The difference is where the font lives: not on a server, but in your browser's storage.

<h2 id="what">What you can do</h2>

- **Draw and edit**: every tool and panel of the editor, path operations (remove overlap, union, subtract), copy and paste, undo.
- **Open your own fonts**: the *Your fonts* bar at the bottom opens a file or a folder from your disk, or starts a **new font** from a Latin glyph set.
- **Export**: *File › Export as* writes a `.fontra`, a designspace with UFOs, or a compiled TrueType (TTF, WOFF2). OTF is not offered.
- **History, snapshots, branches, comments**: Try Fontra includes Fontra Hive working alone, for one person. Every edit is kept; hover a version of a glyph to see it, restore it, name a snapshot; draw on a branch and merge it; pin a comment to a point.

<h2 id="formats">Files and formats</h2>

Try Fontra reads `.fontra`, UFO and designspace, `.glyphs` and `.glyphspackage`, TTF, OTF, WOFF and WOFF2. A UFO, a `.fontra` and a `.glyphspackage` are folders: use *Open a folder…*. The file is converted in your browser and kept there; the one on your disk is never touched.

<h2 id="limits">What stays in your browser</h2>

Your fonts are stored by the browser, on this computer, and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded. That also means: no collaboration in real time, no sharing by link, and one device. Clearing the browser's site data removes the fonts, so export what you want to keep. *Remove*, in *Your fonts*, forgets a font here and does nothing to your files.

For a team — several people on one font, a history that says who changed what, reviews before a merge — the server version is <a href="https://fontrahive.com">Fontra Hive</a>.

<h2 id="tutorials">Tutorials</h2>

The tours and lessons of the <a href="https://github.com/Uplus-Type/fontra-tutorials">Fontra Tutorials</a> plug-in are built into Try Fontra, behind the <strong>?</strong> of the bar: *Around the editor*, *Your first font* (the lesson version of <a href="/tutorials/your-first-variable-font/">Your first variable font</a>), *Font info and designspace*, *The font overview*, *Coming from Glyphs*, *Coming from RoboFont*. A tutorial can be opened directly: <code>https://try.fontrahive.com/try?tour=first-font</code>.
