## Guided tours

Short tours of Fontra's windows, a few minutes each. The same tours run inside the editor, with a bubble on each part of the real interface: the [Fontra Tutorials](https://github.com/Uplus-Type/fontra-tutorials) plug-in (address `uplus-type/fontra-tutorials` in the [Plugin manager](/reference/menu/fontra/plugin-manager)), also built into [Try Fontra](/get-started/try-online/).
