The editor's **Font** menu opens the font overview, in a tab of its own. It is also where Fontra Pak opens a font.

<h2 id="cells">The cells</h2>

<div class="video"><div class="play"></div><div class="cap">double-click a cell, the editor opens on that glyph</div></div>

One cell per glyph, with the empty places of the glyph set in grey. **Double-click a cell** to open that glyph in the editor; a grey one waits there for a double-click to create it.

<h2 id="search">Search</h2>

Type a glyph name or a character at the top of the sidebar: the cells narrow down as you type. Clear the field to see the whole font again.

<h2 id="glyph-sets">Glyph sets</h2>

**Project glyph sets** belong to the font — *GF Latin Core*, say, with the glyphs not drawn yet as empty places. **My glyph sets** are yours, offered in every font you open. Tick the ones to show.

<h2 id="group-by">Group by</h2>

Group the cells by script, category or case to find your way in a large font. Each cell takes the colour of its status, as defined in [Font Info › Status definitions](/tutorials/font-info/#statuses).

<h2 id="location">Source and location</h2>

The cells show the font at one place of the designspace: pick another source, or move the sliders, to see every glyph in Bold, say, or anywhere in between.
